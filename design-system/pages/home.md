# Home — deviations from master

- Hero runs a 7/5 column split at `lg`. The headline sits at `--text-6xl` with `wdth 110`; nowhere
  else on the site uses that width.
- The ledger strip under the hero is the only place four numbers sit on one hairline row. Numbers
  count up once, then never move again.
- The failure narrative is the only pinned section in the project. Pinning exists at `lg` and above
  only, and the same content reads as four stacked blocks below that.
- The lane reader embeds the chart at 340px tall against `--surface`, one step darker than the
  surrounding panel, so the trace reads as an inset instrument window.
- Scenario carousel: inactive slides drop to 62% opacity, never below. Autoplay does not exist.
